Crest is ready.

It stays hidden until you hover at the top center of a clear desktop.

1. Start Crest from the final setup screen or the Start menu.
2. Press Windows+D to minimize your applications.
3. Move the pointer away from the top, then hover at the top center.
4. Select Music, Controls, Timer, Launch, or Shelf from the island.

When you open an application, Crest immediately hides. Background music and timers continue without opening the island.

Right-click Crest in the system tray for Settings, Pause, Resume, or Quit. Settings is a normal application window and hides the island while open.

Starting with Windows is off. You can enable it in Settings. No account, internet connection, SDK, or administrator access is required to use Crest.

To uninstall, use Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Crest. You can keep or delete saved Crest data; the files referenced by the shelf are never deleted.
